Account and Data Deletion

App: Isekai Mercenary (Google Play package com.su.IsekaiMercenary)
Developer: NARU Studio
Effective date: 2026-11-18

This page explains how to delete your Isekai Mercenary game account and its data. You can delete it right away inside the game, or, if you cannot use the app, request deletion by email.

1. Delete inside the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap the gear (Settings) button on the right side of the lobby screen.
  2. Under Account, find the Delete Account row and tap Delete.
  3. Read the notice and tap Next, then tap Delete again on the "Are you sure?" confirmation.
  4. When it finishes, you will see "Your account has been deleted." and return to the title screen.

Your server data is deleted as soon as you confirm the second time. This cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion on the web (email)

You can request deletion by email even if you have uninstalled the app or cannot open it.

  1. If you can, find your player ID in the UID row of the game's Settings screen (tap Copy to copy it).
  2. Email us with the subject "Account deletion request" and include:
    • Your UID (player ID). If you don't know it, leave it out and give as much of the information below as you can.
    • Sign-in method (Guest / Google / Apple)
    • Your in-game nickname and the approximate date you last played
    • The email address we should reply to (only if different from the sender)

Email: rapaelzz9897@gmail.com

Write a deletion request

After confirming that the request comes from the account's user, we delete the account within 30 days of receiving the request and email you the result. We may ask for more information if we cannot identify the account.

5. Please note

  • Deleted accounts, game data, and purchased in-game currency or benefits cannot be restored or refunded. If you have paid items still eligible for a refund, request the refund before deleting.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account data on our servers. Please use one of the methods above.
  • If you sign in again with the same Google/Apple account after deletion, you will start over with a new account.
