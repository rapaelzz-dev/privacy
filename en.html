Privacy Policy

NARU Studio ("we", "us") develops the mobile game Isekai Mercenary (the "Game"). This policy explains what data the Game collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can request deletion.

2. How we use data

  • Create your account, sign you in and restore your progress on a new device
  • Save and sync game data and provide server features (leaderboards, rewards)
  • Verify in-app purchases, prevent duplicate grants and handle purchase inquiries
  • Show rewarded ads and measure ad performance
  • Analyze usage statistics, fix bugs and improve game balance
  • Prevent cheating and payment fraud

4. Advertising and consent

The Game shows Google AdMob rewarded ads. In regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK), a consent form (Google UMP) is shown at launch, and personalized or non-personalized ads are served based on your choice. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

5. Retention

We keep data while your game account exists and delete it promptly when you request account deletion. Purchase records that the law requires us to keep are retained for the legally required period and then deleted. Statistics may be kept in a form that cannot identify you.

6. Requesting account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your game account and related data at any time.

  1. Find your player ID (or account information) in the Game's settings screen.
  2. Email rapaelzz@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request", including your player ID and sign-in method (guest/Google/Apple).
  3. After verifying the request, we delete your account, save data, leaderboard entries and analytics data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

You can send this request even if the Game is no longer installed. Deleted data and purchased in-game items cannot be restored. Uninstalling the Game alone does not delete data stored on our servers.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to it. Contact us at the address below and we will respond without undue delay.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server data is protected by Unity Gaming Services access controls so that only the authenticated player can access it. Secret keys used for purchase verification are kept only in a server-side secret store.
