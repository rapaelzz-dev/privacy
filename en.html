2. How we use data

  • Create your account, sign you in and restore your progress on a new device
  • Save and sync game data and provide server features (leaderboards, rewards)
  • Verify in-app purchases, prevent duplicate grants and handle purchase inquiries
  • Show rewarded ads and measure ad performance
  • Analyze usage statistics, fix bugs and improve game balance
  • Prevent cheating and payment fraud

4. Advertising and consent

The Game shows Google AdMob rewarded ads. In regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK), a consent form (Google UMP) is shown at launch, and personalized or non-personalized ads are served based on your choice. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

6. How data is destroyed

When the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we destroy the data without delay. When you delete your account in the Game, your server game data, protected data and leaderboard entries are deleted immediately, followed by your Unity authentication account (player ID and Google/Apple link). For email requests we do the same within 30 days of verifying the request and also request deletion of your Firebase and Unity analytics data. Data kept under section 5 is stored separately and used only for the stated purpose. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered; we do not keep data on paper.

7. Requesting account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your game account and related data at any time. In the Game, you can delete it immediately under Settings > Account > Delete Account. See the Account and Data Deletion page for full steps and what data is deleted or kept. If you cannot use the app, request deletion by email:

  1. Find your player ID in the UID row of the Game's settings screen.
  2. Email rapaelzz9897@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request", including your player ID and sign-in method (guest/Google/Apple).
  3. After verifying the request, we delete your account, save data and leaderboard entries within 30 days, request deletion of your analytics data, and reply to confirm.

You can send this request even if the Game is no longer installed. Deleted data and purchased in-game items cannot be restored, so if you have paid items that are still eligible for a refund, request the refund before deleting. Uninstalling the Game alone does not delete data stored on our servers. Data kept under section 5 remains for that period after deletion.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to it. Contact us at the address below and we will respond without undue delay.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server data is protected by Unity Gaming Services access controls so that only the authenticated player can access it. Secret keys used for purchase verification are kept only in a server-side secret store.
